# Hams Tavern: The Bells of Lahti

A solo fantasy RPG with an offline game master. You type what you want to do in plain words — *punch the zombie*, *ask Squib about the barrel*, *kick the vestry door* — and the game master resolves it with fifth-edition rules, dice, and a world that remembers how you treated it. No server, no AI service, no account: it all runs on the device.

**Play the playtest build:** https://hamsaladslammer.github.io/hams-tavern/

**Android app (playtest):** https://hamsaladslammer.github.io/hams-tavern/hams-tavern.apk
Open the link on an Android phone, download it, then tap the file to install. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. If Play Protect warns about an unrecognised developer, tap **More details → Install anyway**. Later builds install over the old one and keep your save.

You can also open the web link in Chrome and choose **Add to Home screen**.

## What's new in v0.6: the lore brain

- **People know the world.** Ask anyone about any place, person, faction, god, vice or bit of history in Lahti and they answer from a library of 520+ written facts drawn from the setting: towns, landmarks, the war, the treaty, the bells, the cult, the old faith and more.
- **Who knows what.** An innkeeper in Coltale knows Coltale. A sailor has heard the river rumours. A crown agent knows things she'll only say quietly. Some things are secrets, and story spoilers stay locked until you've earned them.
- **Directions.** "Where is Greymarch Field?" gets a compass direction and rough days on the road, and pins the place on your World map.
- **Follow-ups.** "What else?", "tell me more", "where is it?" and "what is she like?" carry on with the same subject. Nobody repeats themselves.
- **Your own memory.** "What do I know about the war?" lists what you've heard and rolls History, Religion, Arcana or Nature to dredge up more.
- **Journal.** A new "What you've learned" section collects everything people have told you.
- **Report card.** `npm run eval` scores the game master on 300 typical player lines. Before v0.6, 21% of answers were on topic and 30% were generic. Now 97% are on topic and 7% are generic.

## What's new in v0.5

- **Pick your story.** Six campaigns in the same open world:
  - **The Bells of Lahti** (the original).
  - **The Drowned Crown:** smugglers and a dead captain in Largo.
  - **Iron and Ash:** a gun-runner selling a war, out of Tokarev.
  - **The Frost Wyrm of Herstal.**
  - **The Mire Below:** the thing at the bottom of the swamp.
  - **The Velvet Knife:** a murder at court in Suomi.
- **Endless campaigns.**
  - **Random:** a generated campaign with a new villain, threat, goal and tone, which you can reroll before you start.
  - **Your own:** pick the threat, villain, goal, town, tone, length and starting level (1 to 20), and the game writes the rest.
  - **The open road:** no story at all.
  - After any ending, say "seek a new adventure" to start another campaign with the same hero in the same world.
- **Dice tray.** Tap **Dice** to roll any mix of d4 to d100 with modifiers, advantage and disadvantage. Pick the dice colour, number colour and finish (solid, gemstone or metal), and the colour of the mat. You can also type "roll 2d6+3". The game's own d20 rolls tumble in the corner as they happen; this can be switched off.
- **A game master that answers.**
  - **Conversations:** people respond to what you actually say, including small talk, directions with real travel times, opinions, jokes, flirting, requests, and questions about themselves, with yes/no answers from the oracle.
  - **People in the crowd:** unnamed people (the barmaid, the stranger, the drunk) become real when you talk to them, and you can wink at them, hire them or hit them.
  - **Everyday actions:** looking around, announcing things, sharpening a blade, writing a letter and saying how you feel all get real responses instead of an echo.
- **Custom origins.**
  - **Custom background:** three ability boosts (+2/+1 or +1/+1/+1), any feat, and two skills plus a tool, or three skills.
  - **Custom Lineage:** +2 to one ability, a second feat of your choice, darkvision or a skill, and Small or Medium size.
  - Skilled now grants its three extra skills.
- **Colours and settings.** Nine colour themes (Tavern, Parchment, Midnight, Thornwood, Blood Moon, Ash, Northern Ocean, Herstal Snow, High contrast), plus your own accent, background and text colours. Settings are on the title screen too.
- **Delete a campaign** from the in-game Menu, or with the 🗑 next to any save on the title screen.
- **Action bar.** Talk, Move, Do and Me dropdowns (and Fight in combat) list everything that makes sense right now, including the people around you, the ways out and the things you can do. Typing anything you like still works.
- **Update from inside the app.** Menu → *Check for updates* (it also checks by itself). The Android app downloads the new version and switches over without reinstalling, and your save stays where it is. Only changes to the app shell need a new APK, and the app tells you when.

## What's new in v0.4

- **The world of Lahti.** Jared's map of Lahti is the World map (tap **World**). All 19 towns are real places you can travel to, each with a square, an inn with an innkeeper, a market with its own specialty stock (Tokarev Works rifles, Borschardt pistols, Largo "fish"), a watch captain and a notice board. Grog's chowder in Brumandell is as advertised.
- **Discovery.** Dungeons, ruins, barrows, bandit camps, cult hideouts and battlefields are pinned to the map only when you find them: by exploring, from rumours in the inns, off the road, or from a job posting. Places are generated from a seed and scaled to your level; named places from the campaign (Greymarch Field, the Charmwright's Grove, the Sunken Ferry, the Erased Face, the Weeping Glacier, the Old Wyrm's lair) are hand-written.
- **Travel.** Journeys take days. Each day on the road can bring an ambush, a toll, a traveller, or a place you didn't know about. Say "continue" to go on after a fight. Rest each night.
- **Jobs.** Notice boards post clearing contracts, bounties, recoveries and deliveries. Take one, do it, collect in town.
- **The story keeps going.** After the chapel, Act Two: three more bell towers (Coltale, Largo, Borschardt), the Old Vaults under Suomi and the Pale Queen herself, then Zenu at the Greenspire, and something worse than a queen under the Mire. Threats advance on their own clock whether you deal with them or not: the bells, a war with Makrovin, the Drinker Below. Type "threats" to see them.
- **Own a piece of it.** Buy a house, farm, shop, forge or tavern in any town. Name it, build a kitchen, a stage or a still, hire a barkeep, a cook, a bouncer or a bard, work the bar yourself, and collect the takings. Thieves, famous bards, tax men and buyout offers turn up.
- **Hirelings.** Hire a sellsword, rifleman, hedge-mage or brawler in any inn (up to two). They fight beside you, level with you, and want paying every morning.
- **Save slots.** Three characters at once, picked from the title screen.
- **Levels 1 to 20.** Every class to 20 with its features, Extra Attacks, ability score improvements, and three subclasses per class (choose at level 3: "choose Champion"). Spells up to 9th level. A bigger bestiary (ghouls to an adult wyrm) and better loot, including the firearms of the last war.

## What's new in v0.3

- **Battle map.** Every room has a square grid map: walls, doors, furniture, tokens for you, your companions, townsfolk and monsters. Fog of war in dark places. Tap a square to walk, a person to talk, a thing to look at, climb, break or take, and a door to leave.
- **Tactical combat on the grid.** 30 ft of movement (reachable squares are highlighted), melee needs adjacency, ranged attacks have normal and long range, cover from furniture (+2 AC), line of sight, opportunity attacks, Dash, Disengage, flanking-style pack tactics, area spells by distance. "Attack the zombie" walks you there first.
- **Player freedom.** Attack anyone. Fists are a brawl (people get knocked out); a blade is murder. Rob, shoplift, threaten, pickpocket. The Breda militia, bounties, bans and grudges answer instead of the game saying no. Join the Pale Queen's cult if you want to. Ignore the main quest and work odd jobs, play cards, hunt wolves on the south road, or deal with the Thatch farm on the east road.

## What's new in v0.2

- **Try anything.** Drink the mug Squib hands you, sit on a stool, climb the rafters, break a chair and swing the leg, light candles, play cards with the dwarves, forage, track, pray, pickpocket. Anything the story doesn't script gets a skill, a DC and a roll; the oracle decides whether the thing you reach for is actually there.
- **Chained commands:** "take the mug, drink it, then go outside".
- **Drinking:** ale, stout, cider, wine and Trenchfire, with tipsy, drunk and blackout.
- **Improvised weapons:** mugs, bottles, chair legs, rocks, bones, candles.
- **The Forge is back:** the original form-based creator with live preview, standard array, 4d6 or point buy, skills, spells, a life path, look, backstory and kit.
- **All 12 classes**, each with a Kalashscovia twist; **35 species** (the 9 SRD species plus original-named folk in the roles players know); **16 backgrounds** with feats.
- Subclasses arrive with level 3, in a later version.

## What's in v0.1

- **Act One, "The Old Chapel Bell":** the village of Breda, the Smoking Barrel and Squib, the Thornwood March, Kessel's Rest, Doss's pasture, and the Old Chapel's graveyard, nave, vestry, bell tower and crypt. Meet Alistair, a lich who never wanted the party to end. It takes about one to two hours to play.
- **Character creation:** 9 species, 5 classes (Fighter, Rogue, Monk, Wizard, Cleric) and 4 backgrounds, at levels 1–2.
- **Combat:**
  - Turn-based, with initiative, advantage, criticals, death saves, Undead Fortitude, and shoving and grappling.
  - Spells, class features, and companions (Kettle the war hound and Alistair).
  - Firearms that jam and need reloading.
- **Plain-language input:** dozens of verbs map to actions. Punch, kick, knee, elbow, headbutt, bite, slap, poke, karate chop, mule kick and the rest are all unarmed strikes, and each gets narration for that particular blow.
- **Speech judged by how you use it:** "oh shit" is an exclamation and "fuck you, Squib" is an insult, and NPCs react to each in character. Squib gives you one warning, then stops serving you. The dwarves cheer. Hetch the cat says "Language."
- **Oracle:** type `? Is anyone watching me` and a yes/no oracle answers. It uses a chaos factor, Mythic-style.
- **Saving:** the game saves automatically after every move. You can export and import saves, there's a dark and a light theme, and you can change the text size.
- **When you lose a fight:** you wake up at dawn with lighter pockets. The Pale Queen wants witnesses.

## Playtesting

1. Play on the link above.
2. If something breaks, open **Menu → Export save** and send the file along with what you typed.
3. Every push to `main` runs the tests and redeploys automatically within a couple of minutes. To get the newest version, reload, or tap the "new version" toast.

## Development

```sh
npm install
npm run dev      # local dev server
npm test         # parser, profanity and full scripted playthroughs of Act One
npm run build    # production build in dist/
```

### Layout

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `src/engine/` | Rules: dice, characters, combat, oracle, and the game master (`game.ts`). Pure TypeScript with no DOM code. |
| `src/nlp/` | Intent parser, verb lexicon, profanity speech-act classifier, and narration grammar |
| `src/content/` | The world: rooms, NPCs, encounters and travel tables for Act One |
| `src/lore/` | The lore brain: entities, facts, who knows what, and how people answer |
| `src/ui/` | Phone-first interface and the save layer |
| `tests/` | Unit tests, plus headless playthroughs that finish Act One with 8 different characters |

### Road to Android and Steam

The game is a static web app built with `base: './'`, so the same `dist/` folder works in all of these:

- **Web/PWA:** GitHub Pages, as it does now.
- **Android:** the Capacitor project in `android/` is already set up. CI builds a signed APK on every push and publishes it to the `playtest` release. Before going to Google Play, swap the committed playtest key for a private upload key and build an `.aab`.
- **Steam:** wrap `dist/` with Electron. Steamworks (achievements, cloud saves) gets added through steamworks.js.
